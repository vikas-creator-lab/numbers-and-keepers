It happens to almost every business owner

Work gets busy, receipts pile up and the bank feed fills with transactions nobody has coded. The longer it goes, the harder it is to start. That's where we come in.

What a catch-up includes

  • A quick review of where your books stand and what's missing
  • Coding and reconciling every bank account, credit card and loan
  • Matching bills, invoices and receipts, and chasing the gaps with you
  • Fixing duplicates, wrong accounts and old balances that don't add up
  • A clean set of reports for you and your accountant

How it works

  • Review. We look at your software and statements and agree a clear plan before any work starts.
  • Catch up. We work through the backlog month by month and send you short lists of questions, not a wall of jargon.
  • Hand over or keep going. You get tidy books. Many clients then move to monthly bookkeeping so it never builds up again.

We work in Xero, MYOB, QuickBooks and any accounting software. If your records are in spreadsheets or a shoebox, that's fine too.

Questions
How far behind is too far?

No backlog is too big. We have caught up books that were years behind. We work through it month by month and keep you updated as we go.

Do I need to have all my receipts?

No. Bank statements give us most of what we need. We'll give you a short list of the items worth finding and tell you which ones don't matter.

Will this help my accountant at year end?

Yes. Clean, reconciled books make your accountant's or tax agent's job faster and help avoid surprises.
