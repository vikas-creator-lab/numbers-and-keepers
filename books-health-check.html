When a health check makes sense

  • Your reports don't look right, or you don't trust them
  • You've done the books yourself and want a second opinion
  • You're changing bookkeepers or software
  • You're about to apply for finance, bring in a partner or sell
  • Year end is coming and you want to hand your accountant clean books

What we review

  • Bank, credit card and loan reconciliations, and whether they actually match
  • Unreconciled and duplicate transactions, suspense and clearing accounts
  • Coding against your chart of accounts, and whether the chart itself makes sense
  • Debtors and creditors: old invoices, unapplied payments and balances that don't add up
  • Balance sheet accounts that have drifted over time
  • Your setup: bank rules, apps, approvals and where time is being lost

What you get

A short written report with what we found, why it matters and a prioritised fix list. We can make the fixes for you, hand them to your team, or set you up with monthly bookkeeping so it stays right.

We review Xero, MYOB, QuickBooks and any accounting software. The health check covers your bookkeeping records; it isn't an audit or tax advice.

Questions
How long does a health check take?

Most small business files take a few days from when we get access. Larger or messier files take longer, and we'll tell you up front.

Do you need access to our software?

Yes, read-only or advisor access is usually enough. We'll show you how to grant it and remove it afterwards.

What if you find a lot of problems?

That's common and nothing to be embarrassed about. We'll prioritise what matters most and suggest the quickest way to fix it, including a catch-up if needed.
