08 Common questions
What does a bookkeeper do?

A bookkeeper records and reconciles your day-to-day transactions, so your accounts are accurate and you always know where the business stands. We go further, with budgets, forecasts, reviews and automation.

Do I need a bookkeeper if I have an accountant?

Often, yes. Your accountant or tax agent handles tax returns and advice. We keep the books clean all year and give you the numbers to plan with, which makes their job quicker.

Can you help with budgeting and cash flow forecasting?

Yes. We build a practical budget from your real numbers, a rolling cash flow forecast and simple what-if scenarios, then help you keep them current and compare them with what actually happened.

Can you review our books and tell us what's wrong?

Yes. A books health check is a CPA review of your file: we check your reconciliations, coding and balances, find gaps and errors, and give you a clear, prioritised list of fixes. We can make the fixes or hand them to your team.

What can you automate?

Common examples are receipt and bill capture, invoice reminders, bill and expense approvals, bank rules, document filing and recurring reports. If a task is repetitive and follows the same steps each time, it can usually be automated.

How does the automation work?

We start with a short review of your current setup and the admin that takes the most time. Then we optimise what you already have, build automations in the tools you use, test them with you and show your team how they run. Nothing is connected without your approval.

Which accounting software do you work with?

Most of them. We work across Xero, MYOB and QuickBooks Online, as well as Sage, SAP, Oracle and Excel. Whatever you use, we can work with it or help you move to something that suits you better.

My books are months behind. Can you help?

Yes. Catch-up bookkeeping is one of our most common jobs. We'll bring everything up to date, then keep it that way.

Do I need to be nearby?

No. We work online with small businesses anywhere in Australia, and meet in person around Melbourne and Geelong when it helps.
