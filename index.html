Common questions

What does a bookkeeper do?

A bookkeeper records and reconciles your day-to-day transactions, so your accounts are accurate and you always know where the business stands.

Do I need a bookkeeper if I have an accountant?

Often, yes. Your accountant or tax agent handles tax returns and advice. We keep the books clean all year, which makes their job quicker.

Which accounting software do you work with?

Xero, MYOB and QuickBooks Online. If you use something else, ask us and we'll let you know.

My books are months behind. Can you help?

Yes. Catch-up bookkeeping is one of our most common jobs. We'll bring everything up to date, then keep it that way.

Do you work with sole traders?

Yes. We work with sole traders, tradies, freelancers and small teams.

Do I need to be nearby?

No. We can meet in person around Melbourne's west and Geelong, or work fully online.
